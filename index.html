01 About

What I spend my time on

Most of my week splits between three things that turn out to depend on each other: setting technical direction and mentoring the engineers and teams around me, getting agentic AI systems from prototype to something a healthcare enterprise can actually run, and keeping the integration platforms underneath them fast, observable, and boring in the way production systems should be.

Fifteen years of SRE discipline shapes how I approach all three — error budgets and observability aren't an afterthought bolted onto an AI system, they're the same standard I'd hold any critical service to, and the same standard I coach other engineers toward.
